Problem first, technology second

An honest answer, not a sales pitch for a platform

Most AI and data consulting starts from a tool or a vendor. This starts from your problem — a workflow, a system, a decision — and works backward to whatever architecture, platform, or plan actually fits it, including the honest answer that the right move is to do less than you planned.

Grounded in delivery

The same practitioner who has built and shipped these systems, not someone repeating vendor marketing.

Scoped to a deliverable

Every engagement ends in something concrete: a written recommendation, an architecture, or working code — never an open-ended retainer.

No guaranteed outcomes claimed

A clear-eyed technical opinion, including "don't build this," when that's the honest answer.
