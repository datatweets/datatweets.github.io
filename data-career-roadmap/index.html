Written for you if…

Talent is widely distributed; opportunity is not. You don’t need a CS degree, perfect English, or insider knowledge to follow this book.

You’re a complete beginner

You want your first job in data and have no idea where to start. This turns a blank page into a map.

You’re changing careers

From teaching, finance, marketing, sales, ops, support, admin, or engineering — your background is an asset, and this shows you how to translate it.

You’re “confused in the middle”

You can’t quite tell a Data Analyst from a Scientist from an Engineer — or where ML, AI, Analytics, and BI roles fit. Chapter 2 settles it.

You already have a foot in the door

A junior data pro who wants a better role, a specialization, or a raise — and a clearer next 90 days.

Not another hype-filled “break into data” guide

Most guides fail you in one of three ways: they’re hype, they’re vague, or they’re out of date. This one is built to be the opposite.

Honest, not hyped

Concrete, not vague

Scripts, templates, worked examples, checklists, and exact next actions — not just “build a strong portfolio.”

Current for 2026

Every market claim is tied to a named, recent source, and AI is treated as it actually is: a tool that changed the work, not a monster that ended it.

Frameworks that stick

A few simple mental models you carry in your head — the Data Value Line, the 60% Rule, the T-A-R bullet, the Watch-to-Build Ratio.

Questions, answered

Is this book good for a complete beginner?

Yes. It assumes no prior data experience and starts by helping you choose a path, then tells you exactly what to learn first (SQL) and what to skip. It’s also written for career changers and junior professionals who want a clearer next step.

What format is it, and how do I get it?

It’s a downloadable PDF eBook, delivered through Ko-fi. You buy once and download instantly — no subscription, no account on this site required.

Does it cover AI, and is it up to date?

Yes. This is the 2026 first edition. It treats AI honestly — as a tool that changed the daily work and raised the bar for beginners — and threads practical AI literacy through skills, portfolios, and interviews.

Does it work outside the United States?

The guidance is written for international readers in plain English. Salary figures are U.S.-based ranges and clearly labelled as such — scale them to your local market and cost of living.

Will it get me a job by itself?

No honest book can promise that. It gives you a realistic process to follow, test, and adapt — the map and the templates. You still do the building, applying, and interviewing; the book makes each step far clearer.

I already use the free DATATWEETS courses. Is this different?

Yes — and they pair well. The free courses give you structured, hands-on skill practice; the book is the career map around them: choosing a path, building proof, and getting hired.
